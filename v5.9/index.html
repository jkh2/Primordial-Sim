Grazers and Hunters (diet gene)
On: each creature's diet runs from grazer (only algae) to hunter (only other creatures), with omnivores eating both in between. Grazers get more from algae; hunters are built to kill, live longer and breed slower. Off: everyone eats everything, as before v5.4.

Bite Contests (armor and jaws)
On: a bite can fail. Armor, a plated rim that slows the body, blocks bites; jaws, fangs at the front, cut through armor. Both cost energy and start small, so they only grow where they pay. A failed bite stuns the attacker and costs it energy. The Armor and Jaws preset turns this on in Origin of Species. Off: every bite succeeds, as before v5.7.

Split Distance0.30
How far a lineage's genes must drift from its species' founder to split off. It becomes a species of its own, with its own name and its own predators, once it has 10 members.
Reproduction
Divide alone: each parent splits into copies of itself, changed only by mutation. Any two mix genes: a parent with a mate of its own species close by mixes genes with it, and a loner still divides alone. Male and female: babies are born male or female, and only a female carries young, only with a male of her own species close by. Males wear a pale ring.
Mate Choice (females pick the best male)
Rival Males (the stronger wins)
Showy Males (flash like fireflies)
Courtship, with Male and female. Mate Choice: a female ready to breed takes the best male in reach instead of the nearest: the biggest, and with Showy Males the brightest. Rival Males: grown males of a species who meet near a female shove, and the stronger drives the other off for 2 seconds, when no female will take him. A brawn gene lets a male grow up to half again as big and fight harder, at a running cost. Showy Males: grown males flash like fireflies, each species in its own rhythm, and a display gene makes a male brighter. Females see a bright male from farther away and prefer him, but flashing costs energy, and hunters see him from farther away too and go for him first. Females carry both genes without showing them.
Protective Parents (care gene)
Who Guards
After a birth, a caring parent stops feeding and guards the young for up to 6 seconds. The babies follow it like ducklings and it puts itself between them and hunters. When a hunter catches a baby near it, the more caring the guard is the likelier it drives the hunter off, unless the hunter is big enough to eat it too. More care means fewer, stronger babies; less care means many small ones. Who Guards counts with Male and female: the mother, the father or both. With Evolves a gene decides, so each species finds its own way. Only a grown father stays. A guarding father can't mate, so a father who leaves can sire more young.
